# Chuwi Emotional Datasets

## Multimodal Child Emotion for Learning
- Modalities: facial images, speech audio, posture.
- Purpose: multimodal child emotion recognition.
- Use in Chuwi: pretraining/fine-tuning emotional perception.

## Child Voice Emotion Dataset
- Modality: audio.
- Purpose: child speech emotion recognition.
- Use in Chuwi: voice emotion branch.

## Facial Emotion Datasets
- Modality: images/video frames.
- Purpose: facial expression recognition.
- Use in Chuwi: vision emotion branch.

## Note
Raw datasets are not stored in this repository due to size, licensing and privacy considerations. Download and place approved datasets locally following the project configuration.
